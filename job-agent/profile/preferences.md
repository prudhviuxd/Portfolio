# Search preferences

Edit freely - the agent reads this every run and screens against it.

## Target roles

- Head of Design / Design Director / Design Lead
- Principal or Lead Product Designer
- UX Strategy lead roles
- Design Manager, where the team is 5+

Not interested in: individual-contributor-only visual design, pure graphic
design, junior or mid-level IC roles.

## Location and arrangement

- Hyderabad and Bangalore, on-site or hybrid
- Fully remote anywhere, if the role is senior enough to justify it
- Open to relocation for: Singapore, UAE, UK

## Search terms to use

`design lead`, `head of design`, `design director`, `ux strategy`,
`principal product designer`, `design manager`

## Sources to pull each run

- `adzuna` with location `Hyderabad` and `Bangalore` (covers a large share of
  Indian board listings, Naukri-sourced included)
- `remotive` for remote senior design roles
- Target-company ATS boards - add slugs here as you find them, format
  `<ats>:<slug>`, e.g. `greenhouse:figma`, `lever:someco`, `ashby:someco`

## Screening rules

- Score below 60: reject, do not write a packet.
- Score 75 or above: write a packet.
- Hard rejects regardless of score: contract-to-hire under 6 months, roles
  reporting into engineering with no design org, agencies billing hourly.
- Flag but do not auto-reject: comp not stated, team size not stated.
