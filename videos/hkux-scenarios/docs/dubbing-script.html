HKUX Dubbing Script

AI × UX · Design Thinking series, English. 24 Shorts (5.1 already has its voiceover). Read each Short in one take. The time beside each line is where the silent video currently expects it to start.

Recording notes

  • Save each take as the file name on its card, for example 2.3.m4a, and put it in public/vo/.
  • You don't need to hit the times exactly. When your file is aligned, the scenes and captions move to your delivery.
  • The times assume the same pace as the 5.1 voiceover, about 2.9 words a second with short breaths at full stops.
  • Read the words as written: captions use this exact text, so an ad-libbed word will be timed but won't appear on screen.
