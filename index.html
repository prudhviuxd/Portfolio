As a mentor, I grow judgement, not just output.

Mentoring is part of the job, not an extra. Every designer on the team moves through the same rhythm, and beyond the team I teach UI/UX to people starting out. The aim is always the same: designers who can make, and defend, their own calls.

  1. Onboarding

    A structured programme for junior designers, so the team’s standards are taught, not guessed.

  2. Weekly critique

    The work on the wall, the reasoning out loud, and the evidence named before opinions.

  3. 1:1s

    Regular one-to-ones on each designer’s next step: craft, confidence and client conversations.

  4. Ownership

    Designers who lead their own reviews and workshops, and mentor the next junior in turn.
