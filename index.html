02 — Approach

Three convictions that survive contact with delivery.

I

Vision before velocity

Shipping fast is worthless without a shared picture of where the product is going. I set that picture early, with the people who have to build it, so momentum compounds instead of scattering.

II

Designers grow, or the work stalls

A team that only executes will only ever produce what it was told. I mentor toward judgement, so the designers around me make good calls when nobody is watching.

III

Evidence outranks opinion

Taste sets the ceiling; evidence sets the floor. Every significant decision is anchored in research, instrumentation, or a test we agreed on before the argument started.

I

Rhodium-grade craft

Rare, hard, and it does not tarnish. I hold interfaces to that standard, down to the states nobody demos: empty, error, offline, and the slow third-world connection.

II

Research is the raw material

Interviews, analytics and usability tests are not a phase to clear. They are the material the design is cut from, and I keep returning to them while the pixels move.

III

Accessible by default

Contrast, target size, keyboard paths and language are decided at the start, not retrofitted after an audit. Inclusion is cheaper as a constraint than as a fix.
