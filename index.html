How I lead

A team that gets better every quarter.

I run a team of 18 designers and researchers at Tech Mahindra (BORN) and still design hands-on. This is what the job looks like week to week.

  • 18

    Designers & researchers

    Mentored through weekly design critiques, 1:1s and a structured onboarding programme for juniors.

  • 25%

    Faster handoff

    Component-based Figma design systems and a tighter Agile workflow cut design-to-development handoff time by a quarter.

  • 70+

    RFPs shaped

    UX design and strategy owned for more than seventy RFP submissions, from framing the problem to the proposal.

  • WCAG

    One standard

    The UX standards, templates and accessibility checks every deliverable is held to, written by the team, not inherited.

AI in my practice

Usability heuristics agent

Reviews UI designs against Nielsen’s 10 usability heuristics and returns ranked issues, so design reviews start from evidence instead of a blank page.

Agent 02 · Research

AI interview agent

Built on the Anthropic API: runs AI-driven user interviews and turns them into structured insight reports the team can act on the same day.

Product · Conversational UX
