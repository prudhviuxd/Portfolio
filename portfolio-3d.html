02

Approach

How I actually work

Three convictions — the things I will argue for in a room, and the reason teams I lead ship what they said they would.

  • Vision before velocity

    Shipping fast is worthless without a shared picture of where the product is going. I set that early, with the people who have to build it.

  • Designers grow, or work stalls

    A team that only executes produces only what it was told. I mentor toward judgement, so good calls happen when nobody is watching.

  • Evidence outranks opinion

    Taste sets the ceiling, evidence sets the floor. Every significant call is anchored in research, instrumentation, or a test agreed before the argument.

  1. Phase 01

    Discovery & strategy

    UX research · UX strategy · KPI metrics · Service blueprints · Analytics · Stakeholders · Workshops · RFPs · AI co-pilot

  2. Phase 02

    Design & build

    Estimation · Design oversight · Cross-functional · Dev handover

  3. Phase 03

    Test & ship

    KPI measurement · Beta testing · Design QA

  • Craft that holds up

    Rare, hard, and it does not dull. I hold interfaces to that down to the states nobody demos: empty, error, offline, slow connection.

  • Research is raw material

    Interviews, analytics and usability tests are not a phase to clear. They are what the design gets cut from, and I keep going back while pixels move.

  • Accessible by default

    Contrast, target size, keyboard paths and language get decided at the start, not retrofitted after an audit. Inclusion is cheaper as a constraint.
