## Platform

web

## Users
Hiring managers, design leaders and recruiters evaluating Prudhvi Raj PJ for UX Leadership roles. They skim in seconds, often between other candidates, and decide whether to open a case study or get in touch.

## Product Purpose
Personal portfolio of a UX Leader (Tech Mahindra / BORN, Hyderabad, 12 years in product design). Success: a reviewer grasps the role and value at once, opens a case study, downloads the CV or sends an enquiry.

## Positioning
A design leader who still designs hands-on: leads and mentors a team of 18 designers and researchers, has shipped for WHO, MTN, Citi and the IPL, runs design-thinking workshops (30+, including Singapore Land Authority and Saudi Esports Federation) and builds AI agents for UX work.

## Operating Context
Viewed on desktop during hiring reviews and on phones from shared links (LinkedIn, email). Static HTML/CSS/JS uploaded as a zip to shared hosting with a PHP contact form; no build step.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Static files only; third-party scripts must be pinned and loadable from a CDN or self-hosted.
- Homepage hero must keep: the headline "UX Leader advancing UX maturity / Innovation / AI", the two CTAs (See selected work, Start a conversation), the summary line, the tools row and the availability line.
- Email stays prudhviuxd@gmail.com.

## Brand Commitments
Existing identity: near-black #0d0d0d ground, amber #e8a165 accent, ember #a9531d, sand #e3d4bf / #f6f0e7; Familjen Grotesk display, Instrument Sans body, Martian Mono labels; four-point star ornament; stars and falling stars in the hero.

## Evidence on Hand
Case studies: WHO mYoga, My MTN, IPL Mobile. CV PDF in /cv. Portrait in assets/img. No testimonials yet (section built, hidden); do not fabricate quotes, clients or metrics.

## Product Principles
- Clarity first: role and value readable in the first second, before any motion.
- Craft as evidence: the site itself should show judgement, not spectacle.
- Truthful proof only: every number must trace to a source.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA: text contrast over any animated background, prefers-reduced-motion respected, a pause control for motion longer than 5 seconds, keyboard reachable.
