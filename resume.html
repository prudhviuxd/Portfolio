Professional summary

UX Leader and hands-on UX/UI designer with 12 years of experience and 100+ projects designing enterprise web and mobile applications for healthcare, banking, telecom, logistics and e-commerce clients.

I focus on understanding users and the business problem first, through interviews (100+ conducted), usability sessions and product data, and then make that understanding visible to the team as journey maps, information architecture and user flows before any UI work starts in Figma. Comfortable owning a product area end to end with minimal oversight, from discovery and design through developer handoff and post-launch measurement.

Accessibility (WCAG 2.1) and measurable outcomes are part of how I judge whether a design is finished. I have run 30+ design thinking workshops with clients, including on-site sessions in Singapore and Riyadh, and I have built AI agents for usability evaluation and user research.
