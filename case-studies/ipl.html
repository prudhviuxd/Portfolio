UX Case Study · Mobile · Tata IPL

Designing the IPL app for a billion cricket moods

How demographic research reshaped a live-cricket mobile app for superfans, casual watchers, and a new generation discovering the game on their phones.

  • Live scores
  • Fantasy
  • Multilingual
  • Lite mode
The 30-second readJump to impact
Problem
One app for the superfan, the casual watcher and the new fan, many of them in Tier-2/3 cities on patchy networks, all opening it at the same moment on match day.
Key decision
Design to three researched personas instead of an “average” user, with a Lite mode for weak networks and one-tap clip sharing for new fans.
  • Results after one season
  • +42%match-day retention after one season
  • 18maverage session, up from 11 minutes
  • 61%Lite-mode adoption among Tier-2/3 users

Demographic Research

Who watches the IPL — and how they watch

We synthesized 1,200 survey responses, 18 interviews, and in-app analytics. Three patterns stood out: a young, mobile-first audience; rapid growth beyond metro cities; and huge diversity in language and cricket fluency.

68%watch on mobile as their primary screen
40%of viewers are women — a fast-rising segment
2 of 3new users prefer a language other than English
Personas

Three fans, three very different needs

🏆

Rahul Menon

The Superfan
  • Age · 26
  • Bengaluru · Metro
  • Flagship Android · 5G

Watches every match, runs a fantasy league with college friends, and second-screens live stats while the TV plays in the background.

Goals

  • Ball-by-ball live data without refreshing
  • Beat friends in fantasy & predictions
  • Deep stats: matchups, win probability

Frustrations

  • Score updates lag behind the TV feed
  • Fantasy & scores live in separate apps
  • Notifications are noisy and untimed
📱

Priya Sharma

The Casual Watcher
  • Age · 32
  • Indore · Tier-2
  • Mid-range Android · patchy 4G

Follows her home team during playoffs. Wants the gist fast — who’s winning, key moments — without cricket jargon or clutter.

Goals

  • Glanceable score in under 3 seconds
  • Catch highlights she missed
  • Follow one team, skip the rest

Frustrations

  • Dense stat screens feel overwhelming
  • Heavy pages stall on weak networks
  • Too much English-only terminology
✨

Arjun Patel

The New Fan
  • Age · 19
  • Surat · Tier-2
  • Budget Android · Hindi-first

Got into cricket through reels and memes. Learning the rules, loves short-form clips, shares moments in group chats.

Goals

  • Understand what’s happening in plain language
  • Short, shareable video moments
  • Learn players & teams over time

Frustrations

  • Assumes prior cricket knowledge
  • No easy way to share clips
  • English-only onboarding
Challenge → Solution

Mapping persona pain to design decisions

Every design decision traces back to a real, prioritized problem from research.

Challenge · SuperfanLive scores lag & require manual refresh
→
Real-time score engine + live win-probabilityA persistent live bar with sub-second push updates and an animated win-probability meter, so second-screen users stay ahead of the broadcast.
Challenge · SuperfanFantasy and scores split across apps
→
Unified match hub with fantasy inlineYour fantasy XI, points, and leaderboard sit inside the same match screen — one tab away from live commentary.
Challenge · Casual · New fanStat screens feel dense & intimidating
→
Progressive disclosure & ‘Simple / Pro’ modesA glanceable summary card by default; advanced analytics tuck behind an optional Pro toggle so casual fans never see clutter.
Challenge · Tier-2/3 fansHeavy pages stall on weak networks
→
Lite mode & data-aware loadingSkeleton-first rendering, compressed media, and a Lite mode that drops to text + key frames on 3G — designed for patchy connectivity.
Challenge · New fan · Tier-2/3English-only onboarding excludes many
→
Multilingual UI + plain-language commentaryHindi-first onboarding with 6 language options and a jargon-free commentary mode that explains terms inline.
Challenge · New fanNo easy way to share moments
→
One-tap clip & meme sharingAuto-generated 15-second highlight clips with a share sheet tuned for WhatsApp and Instagram groups.
The Design

The screens, mapped to fan needs

From the personalized home to a real-time Match Center, fantasy, and player profiles — each screen is tagged with the persona it serves most. Hover a phone to scroll the full screen.

  • Real-time first

    Sub-second score pushes and a live win-probability meter keep second-screen superfans ahead of the broadcast.

  • Progressive disclosure

    A Simple/Pro toggle hides advanced analytics until asked for, so casual fans never feel overwhelmed.

  • Built for every network

    Lite mode, skeleton loading, and compressed media make match day work on patchy 3G in Tier-2/3 cities.

Impact

Results after one IPL season

Designing to distinct personas — rather than an “average” user — lifted engagement across every segment, with the biggest gains among the fast-growing Tier-2/3 and new-fan audiences.

+42%Match-day retentionD1 return during live games
18mAvg. session lengthup from 11m
61%Lite-mode adoptionof Tier-2/3 users
2.3×Fantasy attach ratevs. previous season

Key takeaway

India’s cricket audience is not a monolith. By grounding the design in real demographic and behavioral segments — age, geography, language, and fandom intensity — we shipped a single app that feels personal to a superfan in Bengaluru and a first-time fan in Surat alike.
